Solidus Commodities DMCC

The firm

Solidus Commodities DMCC is a merchant and broker of physical commodities, incorporated in the Dubai Multi Commodities Centre. The firm buys, sells and connects verified supply to vetted buyers, and is licensed to import, export and sell.

Counterparties are onboarded before commerce. Consignments move on documentation of origin, assay and chain of custody, or they do not move.

The firm takes its name from the solidus, the gold coin struck under Constantine from A.D. 312. It held its weight and purity for seven centuries.

Commodities

Physical trade in six lines, sourced against documentation and delivered to specification.

01
GOLD

Doré from verified producers; refined bar to market specification. Traded on assay and provenance.

02
COPPER

Cathode, wire rod and concentrate to industrial specification.

03
COBALT

Metal briquette and hydroxide, sourced under due diligence appropriate to origin.

04
COAL

Thermal and metallurgical grades, contracted on calorific value, ash and sulphur.

05
FERTILISER

Urea, potash and phosphates in bulk, delivered to season and contract.

06
SULPHUR

Granulated, in bulk, contracted on specification and laycan.
